**Status:** draft v2, updated Oct 31, 2026\. Footprint review changes F1–F6 approved by Kyron on Oct 31\. E-Deep Effort Range is PAUSED until the deep effort indicator is rebuilt from Jeron's screenshots and checked; Phase 5 runs E-Footprint\_Imbalances alone for now. Applies to Book 1A only.

## **Why two entry arms**

> * Jeron has one thought process at every entry: at a location, in context, one side is trapped (lots of aggression with no reward, or no aggression at all) and the other side steps in. Part 3: "not mechanical in entry, but we are mechanical in thought process" (00:34:58–00:35:40, also 00:19:55). Part 5: "entry model without narrative and framing is useless" (00:15:46–00:15:50).  
> * He reads that thought process on two chart setups and says they show the same thing: "On deep charts, it would look like a effort zone and deep trades, right? Imbalances all the same" (Part 3, 00:14:53); "It's all the same thing" (00:16:55–00:17:00).  
> * The two setups give different mechanical entries, stops and trade management. S1.4 only encodes the footprint version. Testing both in one arm would hide which one works, so each gets its own arm.

## **Shared rules (both arms; logged only until tested)**

> * **VWAP initiation grade.** Entry at the level only \= B+; entry on a power trade, effort zone or imbalance through VWAP \= A+. Part 3, 00:12:00–00:12:40 ("I don't want to sell until I see selling effort through VWOP"), 00:30:05–00:30:35 ("I'd say this was a B+… Now we have an A+ trade"). Logged as a tag on every trade.  
> * **Re-entry.** After an exit, no new entry the same way until the opposing effort zone or imbalance has been run through. Part 3, 00:19:10–00:19:35 ("I don't want to be in shorts again till this is ran through"), 00:40:55–00:41:05.  
> * **Stop and reverse.** If the stop is hit and the other side initiates (power trade, imbalance or deep effort through the level), that candle is a new entry the other way with a tight stop at it. Part 3, 00:31:00–00:31:55 ("that's the initiation candle… I can still have a really tight stop").  
> * **No trade in a fight.** When buying and selling zones are both holding at the level, no entry. Part 2, 01:09:44–01:10:07; Part 3, 00:10:55 (doji: "there's a fight at this level").  
> * **Wait for the response.** The trapping print alone is not the entry; the next bar where the other side steps in is. Part 3, 00:31:05 ("I'm not just buying immediately with this power trade… it's the next candle where buyers step in").  
> * **Counter-context needs more proof** (already in Phase 3 side rules as INITIATION). Part 4, 00:10:28–00:10:52.  
> * **Entry must match the trade idea and target set before entry.** Part 2, 01:11–01:14.  
> * R at entry (from Phase 4, approved Oct 31): at the entry bar, R is measured with the actual entry stop (the arm's stop) to TP1, TP2 and the runner of the Phase 6 ladder, and logged on every candidate. Like the other shared rules, the R minimums (ours, needed; for example TP1 1.5 R or runner 3 R) only block a trade once they are tested. Size is set to a fixed risk per trade. Jeron: Webinar, 2:08 ("I put my stop where my trade is invalidated… sized lighter"); Part 1, 00:20, 01:10 (2:1 at TP1, 3 R at TP2, 5:1 to the weekly value area high); Part 2, 01:28–01:29 (the same idea is 2 R or 4 R depending on the entry and stop).
